🌨 About Snow Day Calculator

Snow Day Calculator is a free online tool that helps students, parents, and teachers estimate the probability of school closures due to winter weather. Using snowfall forecasts and weather conditions, it delivers quick predictions to help everyone prepare ahead of time.
